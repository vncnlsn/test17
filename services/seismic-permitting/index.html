Landowner contact and conditions

Every property presents its own conditions. Access routes, crops and livestock, hunting seasons, fences, water wells, infrastructure, environmental restrictions, compensation and potential damages may affect field operations. APS contacts landowners personally, documents negotiated conditions and carries them into field oversight.

Approvals and administration

APS coordinates applicable municipal, county, state and federal approvals, tracks permits and manages landowner payments, required IRS filings and surface damage coordination.

Early involvement

Preliminary ownership research can reveal divided interests and difficult access before significant resources are committed.
