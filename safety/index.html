Field safety practices

APS operates under its Safety Program Manual and Injury and Illness Prevention Program. Personnel participate in daily safety briefings and job safety analyses, monitor compliance and exercise stop-work authority.

Contractor management

APS reports ISNetworld membership. Membership does not establish approval by any particular operator. Current safety and insurance documentation should be requested directly from APS.

Vendor qualification

For current insurance, HSE records, training and contractor-management documentation, contact APS leadership.
